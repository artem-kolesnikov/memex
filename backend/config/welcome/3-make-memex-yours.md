## Skills

A skill is a note tagged `skill`: a set of instructions an assistant loads when a task
matches. Every connected assistant sees the list of your enabled skills with their
descriptions, and opens one when the task in front of it fits. The same skill works in every
assistant you connect.

Two are here already, and switched on:

- [[skill — plain writing]] strips the habits that make text read as machine-written. Try it in a
  connected assistant:

  > Draft an email asking my landlord to fix the heating.

- [[skill — handoff]] saves where a piece of work stands and picks it up again, in any assistant.
  At the end of a working chat, say:

  > Save where we are.

  Later, in the same assistant or another one:

  > Pick up [the name of the work].

Open either note to see what the assistant is told. Edit it, and the next assistant to load
it gets the new text. The [Skills]({{base}}/skills) page lists your skills, switches each
one on or off, limits one to particular connections, and shows how often each was loaded.
Below yours are the skills built into memex, which every assistant gets.

**Make your own.** Ask a connected assistant:

> Make me a memex skill for [the task].

memex gives it built-in instructions for writing a skill, and it proposes one; the new skill
waits in your Review inbox, and once you approve it every assistant can use it. You can also
write one by hand on the Skills page.

## Your profile

Your profile is a note about you, tagged `user-profile`: your work, your interests, and how
you like answers. memex tells every connected assistant to read it before answering anything
that depends on who you are, so you do not have to explain yourself in every chat.

You have none yet. Start one in [Settings › Personalization]({{base}}/settings/personalization),
under *About you*: *Add profile* walks you through a few choices, or ask an assistant to help
write it. It is a note like any other; edit it whenever something changes.

## How assistants write notes here

memex supplies writing rules for notes, so that a note an assistant saves is easy to read and
to find later: what to put first, how long a note should be, how to record a decision or a
meeting. Choose their presets in Settings › Personalization, or switch to your own writing
rules there.
