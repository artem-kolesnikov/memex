Memex is a web app where your AI can save decisions, reasoning and project context as
notes. You can read and edit every note, then use it with any assistant you connect. As your
work changes, ask your assistant to update the notes. Review what it suggests and decide what
stays.

Ask Claude, ChatGPT or whatever you use; it reads your memex and answers from your notes,
and it saves what you decide here, where every assistant you connect can find it again.

## Where to start

1. [[Connect your first assistant]]: a few minutes in your assistant's settings, then one
   question to check that it reached memex.
2. [[Make memex yours]]: skills, your profile, and how assistants write notes here. Two
   skills are already switched on, so you can try one in your first chat.

## What happens to what an assistant writes

By default, nothing an assistant writes changes your memex until you say so. A new note
waits in your [Review inbox]({{base}}/inbox), and so does every edit, deletion and merge.
Approve, amend or reject each one there. Every note says who added it and who changed it
last.

When you trust a connection to work unattended, make it a curator in
[Settings › Assistants]({{base}}/settings/connections): its new notes and edits then apply
at once and are recorded in the Activity log, while deletions and merges still wait for you.

## Your notes

Write a note yourself with *New note*, or import Markdown files from
[Settings › Notes]({{base}}/settings/content), which also exports everything as Markdown
files that open in Obsidian. Put another note's title in double square brackets to link to
it, as these notes do; each note lists the notes that link to it.

## Questions about memex

Ask any connected assistant. memex's documentation is built in as the `memex-docs` skill, and
an assistant answers questions about memex from it.
