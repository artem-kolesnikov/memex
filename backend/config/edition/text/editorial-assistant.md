**Editorial assistant.** *Analyze draft* checks the draft against what you already have:
possible duplicates with a similarity score, notes the text names but does not link (one
click inserts the link), and tags from your vocabulary the text uses. These checks run on
your own data and cost nothing. It also offers a stronger title and a description when
descriptions are on for you, on your own provider key. One sentence in the card says which:
that they need your own key (with a link to Settings › AI features), or that they use your
own key. Nothing is applied until you click it, and nothing is saved until you save.
