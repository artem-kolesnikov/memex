**Personal → Account**
- *Your details*: your picture and name.
- *Password*: the current password and a new one. Changing it signs out every other
  browser.
- *Signed-in browsers*: sign out one browser or all the others.
- *Delete my account*: permanent and immediate. Type your email to confirm. Notes,
  history, deleted notes, tags, connections and the journal are destroyed, with no grace
  period, and the server returns to its first run. A link above the delete button takes you
  to Notes → Export.
