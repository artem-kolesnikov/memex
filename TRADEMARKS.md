# The memex name and logo

The code in this repository is free software under the GNU Affero General Public License
([LICENSE](LICENSE)). The name **memex** as the name of this software, the memex wordmark
and the memex logo are not covered by that licence. They belong to Artem Kolesnikov, and
this policy says how they may be used.

## Without asking

- Run memex, changed or not, for yourself, with its name and logo where the software shows
  them.
- Offer memex to others under its name, unchanged except for its configuration.
- Say in words that what you made works with memex or is based on memex.
- Write about memex, review it or teach it, with the name and the unaltered logo.

## Only with written permission

- Offering a changed memex to others under the memex name or logo. A fork offered to others
  replaces them where the software shows them, and may say it is based on memex.
- Using the name or logo in the name of a product, service, company, domain or app store
  listing.
- Altering the wordmark or the logo.
- Anything that suggests Artem Kolesnikov or memex.tools endorses, sponsors or runs what
  you offer.

To ask, open an issue in this repository.
